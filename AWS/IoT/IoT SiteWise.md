AWS IoT SiteWise is a managed service that helps you collect, organize, and analyze data from industrial equipment at scale. It enables you to model assets like machines, processes, or production lines and automatically ingest data from sensors and gateways into the cloud. SiteWise helps you create virtual representations of your physical systems, compute metrics in near real-time, and visualize operational data through built-in dashboards. This allows industrial companies to monitor performance, identify inefficiencies, and make informed decisions without building custom data collection or analytics infrastructure.

Documentation: [IoT SiteWise Reference](https://aws.amazon.com/iot-sitewise/)

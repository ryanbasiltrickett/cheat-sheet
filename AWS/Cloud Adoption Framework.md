The Cloud Adoption Framework (CAF) is a structured approach that helps organizations plan and guide their cloud transformation journey. It identifies key areas—called **perspectives**—that need to be addressed for a successful migration. Each perspective outlines best practices, roles, and processes to support cloud readiness and long-term success. By aligning technology with business objectives, the CAF helps organizations reduce risks, build capabilities, and accelerate cloud adoption.

Documentation: [Cloud Adoption Framework Reference](https://aws.amazon.com/cloud-adoption-framework/)
___
### Overview
#### Key Perspectives
- Business
- People
- Governance
- Platform
- Security
- Operations
#### Business Perspective  
Focuses on ensuring that cloud adoption aligns with business objectives and delivers measurable value. It includes areas like business case development, ROI analysis, strategic planning, and stakeholder alignment to ensure cloud efforts drive business outcomes.
#### People Perspective  
Addresses workforce readiness and change management. It emphasizes building cloud skills, redefining roles and responsibilities, and fostering a culture that supports innovation and agility. It helps ensure your teams are equipped to operate effectively in a cloud environment.
#### Governance Perspective 
Concentrates on managing cloud usage with proper control and visibility. This includes establishing policies, budgeting practices, compliance, and risk management. It ensures cloud resources are used responsibly and in line with organizational standards.
#### Platform Perspective 
Deals with the technical foundation of your cloud environment. It includes designing scalable, reliable, and secure cloud infrastructure, choosing the right services and tools, and setting up environments that support application modernization and agility.
#### Security Perspective 
Ensures the confidentiality, integrity, and availability of data and systems. It focuses on implementing identity and access controls, threat detection, data protection, incident response, and compliance with security standards and regulations.
#### Operations Perspective  
Covers the day-to-day management and monitoring of cloud resources. It involves automation, observability, incident management, backup strategies, and performance tuning to maintain operational health and support continuous improvement.

Amazon Connect is a cloud-based contact center service that enables businesses to provide customer support through voice, chat, and tasks. It is easy to set up, scalable, and integrates with other AWS services to support automation, AI-driven insights, and data analytics. Amazon Connect allows you to create personalized customer experiences using features like intelligent routing, real-time and historical analytics, and chatbot integration through Amazon Lex. With pay-as-you-go pricing and no infrastructure to manage, it's a cost-effective solution for organizations looking to modernize their customer service operations.

Documentation: [Connect Reference](https://aws.amazon.com/connect)

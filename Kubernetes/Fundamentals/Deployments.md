A Deployment in Kubernetes is a controller that manages the lifecycle of applications by defining how many replicas of a pod should run and ensuring they remain in the desired state. It provides declarative updates, allowing you to scale, update, or roll back applications without downtime. Deployments automatically replace failed pods and ensure that the correct version of the application is always running. They are ideal for managing stateless applications and ensure high availability by maintaining the desired number of pods across the cluster.

Documentation: [Deployments Reference](https://kubernetes.io/docs/concepts/workloads/controllers/deployment/)
